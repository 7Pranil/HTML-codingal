Cristiano Ronaldo

The Legend of CR7

Quick Facts

  • Full Name: Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro
  • Date of Birth: February 5, 1985
  • Place of Birth: Funchal, Madeira, Portugal
  • Position: Forward
  • Current Club: Al Nassr
  • Jersey Number: 7

Early Life & Youth Career

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. Growing up in a working-class family, Ronaldo developed a deep passion for football at a young age. He began playing for local club Andorinha before joining Nacional, and eventually landed a trial with Sporting CP at just 12 years old.

Professional Club Career

Ronaldo's senior breakthrough came at Sporting CP, where his explosive pace and skills caught the eye of legendary manager Sir Alex Ferguson. In 2003, he signed with Manchester United, inheriting the iconic #7 shirt. During his first spell at United, he won three Premier League titles and his first UEFA Champions League trophy in 2008, alongside his first Ballon d'Or.

In 2009, Ronaldo transferred to Real Madrid for a then-world-record transfer fee of €94 million. His tenure in Spain established him as one of the greatest scorers in history, becoming Real Madrid’s all-time top goalscorer with 450 goals and securing four additional Champions League titles.

He later moved to Juventus in 2018, winning two Serie A titles, made a celebrated return to Manchester United in 2021, and joined Saudi Arabian club Al Nassr in 2023.

International Stardom

As captain of the Portugal National Team, Ronaldo is the all-time leading international goalscorer in world football. He led Portugal to historic victories at the UEFA European Championship in 2016 and the inaugural UEFA Nations League in 2019.

Major Career Achievements

  • 5x Ballon d'Or Winner (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017)
  • 5x UEFA Champions League Winner
  • 4x European Golden Shoe Winner
  • All-Time Top Scorer in UEFA Champions League History
  • All-Time Top International Goalscorer
